# Register & Sign In

> Sign up with email & password, or sign in with one of several third-party OAuth providers. If this is your first time, please register an account first.

## Registration

### Registration flow

1. Visit the home page and click the **Get API Key** button, or go directly to `/sign-up`

![Register page](/imgs-en/register.jpeg)

2. Fill in the registration details:
   - **Username**: your login name. English is recommended; usernames cannot be changed after registration
   - **Email**: used to receive verification codes (required)
   - **Password**: your login password (8–20 characters; letters and digits are recommended)
   - **Phone** (optional): required on platforms that enable phone verification
3. Click **Create account** to finish creating your account

::: tip Best practices
- Enable [two-factor authentication](../guide/personal-setting.md#two-factor-authentication-2fa) to add an extra layer of security to your account
- Change your password regularly; every 3–6 months is a good practice
- Be careful on public networks and avoid signing into important accounts over public WiFi
- Keep your account safe — never share your password or verification codes with anyone
:::

## Sign In

### Email & password sign-in

1. Visit the home page and click the **Sign In** button in the top-right corner, or go directly to `/sign-in`

![Login page](/imgs-en/login.jpeg)

2. Enter your username and password and click **Sign In**

### Third-party OAuth sign-in

If the platform has configured third-party sign-in, click the icon of the corresponding provider below the sign-in form (GitHub, Discord, LinuxDO, Telegram, OIDC, etc.). After completing authorization on the provider's page, you will be signed in automatically.

### Forgot password

If you have forgotten your password, click the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in page. Enter your registered email and the system will send you a reset link; click the link to set a new password, and the old one becomes invalid immediately.

## Support

If you run into any problems while registering or signing in, you can get help from [Contact Support](../support/contact.md).
