# Quota & Top-Up

> Quota is the platform's internal billing unit: consumption = actual tokens used × model ratio. Click **Wallet** in the left navigation, or go directly to `/wallet`.

::: warning Compliance notice
Top-ups, redemption codes, and referral links are intended for legitimate, authorized scenarios. When using them, comply with your authorized scope, the upstream service terms, platform rules, and the laws and regulations of your location.
:::

![Wallet page](/imgs-en/wallet.jpeg)

Payment methods are configured by the administrator under **System Settings → Payment Settings**.

## Account Statistics

The top of the page shows your account's core metrics in real time:

- **Current Balance**: available account balance
- **Total Usage**: cumulative amount consumed
- **API Requests**: total number of API requests

## Top-Up Methods

You can add account quota via online payment or a redemption code.

| Method | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Redemption code | Enter a redemption code generated by the admin to add quota directly |
| EPay | Domestic aggregated payment (Alipay, WeChat, etc.) |
| Stripe | International credit card payment |
| Creem / Waffo | International payment platforms |

## Top Up via Online Payment

### Choose the top-up amount

In the **Add Funds** area, pick a preset amount or enter a custom amount manually.

### Complete the payment

1. Choose a payment method (EPay / Stripe / Creem / Waffo)
2. Click the **Top Up** button; the page redirects to the corresponding payment platform
3. After completing the payment on the payment platform, you're automatically redirected back and your account balance is updated

## Top Up with a Redemption Code

### Enter the redemption code

1. Paste or type the redemption code provided by the admin into the **Redeem** input field
2. Click the **Redeem Quota** button; the system validates the redemption code
3. On success, the page shows the amount of quota redeemed and your account balance is updated

## Subscription Plans

In the **Subscription Plans** area of the Wallet page, you can browse and subscribe to plans billed by cycle. See [Subscription Plans](../guide/subscription.md).

## Referral Rewards

Every account has a unique referral link. When someone signs up successfully through your link, you earn a referral reward.

### Get your referral link

In the **Referral Program** area of the Wallet page, copy your personal referral link.

### How referral rewards work

1. **Rewards on signup**: after someone uses your referral link and signs up successfully, you (the referrer) immediately receive the referral reward quota configured in the system (credited in a single lump sum).
2. **Transfer to balance**: click the **Transfer to Balance** button to move your accumulated referral quota into your main account balance for use.

## Use Cases

### Scenario 1: Using a redemption code

1. Go to the Wallet page
2. Check your current balance and total usage
3. Redeem quota with a redemption code (if you have one)

### Scenario 2: Earning from referrals

1. Copy your referral link
2. Share it with friends or on social media
3. After a friend signs up, you earn the reward
4. Check your earnings statistics regularly
5. Transfer the rewards to your account balance for use

## Core Value

1. **Transparent balance**: check your account balance and consumption in real time
2. **Complete records**: full spending records make reconciliation easy
3. **Referral incentives**: invite friends to earn extra rewards
4. **Earnings management**: clear earnings statistics and transfer features
