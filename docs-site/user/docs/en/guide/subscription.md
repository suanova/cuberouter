# Subscription Plans

> A subscription is a plan purchased by cycle. Once purchased, you enjoy the plan's quota or privileges for its validity period — ideal for users with stable usage needs.

Subscription plans offer quota packages billed by cycle — you can choose from daily, weekly, monthly, and other cycle options.

## Browse Subscription Plans

In the **Subscription Plans** area of the **Wallet** page, you can view all available plans, including plan name, price, validity period, included quota, and more.

![Subscription plans](/imgs-en/wallet.jpeg)

- **My Subscriptions**: shows the subscriptions currently active on your account and their validity periods
- **Priority Wallet**: choose to prioritize using the quota of a specific subscription, avoiding it being mixed with your account balance
- **Priority subscription preferences**: saved preferences take priority on every call

## How to Subscribe

### Browse plan details

Browse the available plans in the **Subscription Plans** area and check each plan's price, validity period, and included quota.

### Subscribe now

1. Select the plan you want to subscribe to
2. Click the **Subscribe Now** button
3. Complete the payment as prompted
4. Once subscribed, the plan's quota takes effect immediately and you can view it under **My Subscriptions**

## Check Your Current Subscription Status

After purchasing a subscription, you can view the details of your current plan in the **My Subscriptions** area:

- Plan name and expiration date
- Remaining quota in the plan

## Auto-Renewal Settings

You can set here whether to auto-renew after the plan expires.

::: tip Note
Subscription quota and account balance are calculated separately. When **Priority Wallet / Priority Subscription** is configured, calls consume the subscription's quota first and fall back to the account balance once it's exhausted.
:::
